02 — About

Who I am.

12th Grade · Mission San Jose High School
My name is Arnav Gupta, and I am a senior at Mission San Jose High School with a passion for photography and visual storytelling. I first picked up a camera this year and quickly fell in love with portrait and street photography as I love capturing real, unplanned moments that tell a story without words. Outside of photography, I love speedcubing and chess — just puzzles in general! Through this class I've learned manual camera settings, natural lighting, and Lightroom editing, and I'm most proud of my community voices project capturing my fellow peers and teachers on campus. Next year, I'm off to UC San Diego and though I will be studying biology in hopes to attend medical school, I'll always keep shooting photos on the side and making it a large part of my college experience and beyond!

Portfolio · 01

Event Photography.

Capturing the beauty and variety of vehicles on display at a local car show, from sleek modern sports cars to meticulously restored classics.

Written Component

About This Project

This collection captures the beauty and variety of vehicles on display at a local car show, from sleek modern sports cars to meticulously restored classics. I focused on the details that make each car unique — the curves of the bodywork, the gleam of polished chrome, and the bold colors that draw the eye. Shooting the vehicles up close allowed me to experiment with angles and framing to highlight each car as its own work of art.

About This Project

Portfolio · 03

Photo Essay.

An exploration of the interactions between humans and nature, capturing their dynamic coexistence.

Written Component

About This Project

This collection explores the delicate relationship between humans and the natural world, capturing moments where our impact on the environment is both visible and deeply felt. I focused on the details that tell the story of climate change — the contrast between urban sprawl and struggling greenery, the beauty of nature persisting despite human interference, and the quiet urgency in scenes we often overlook. Photographing this theme challenged me to find powerful compositions that spark conversation about how we treat the planet we share.
